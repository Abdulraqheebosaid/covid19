Abstract



In the era of digital communication, social media platforms like Twitter have become a central hub for the public to express their sentiments, especially during global crises such as the COVID-19 pandemic. The vast amount of data generated through tweets provides a fertile ground for extracting valuable insights into public sentiment, presenting an opportunity to leverage machine learning techniques for sentiment analysis. This research addresses the challenge of understanding public opinion and sentiment towards the COVID-19 pandemic by developing a sentiment analyser tailored for COVID-19 tweets.
